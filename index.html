ProofVault

A tamper-evident agreement and dispute-prevention tool for buyers, tenants, artisans and freelancers.

Problem: Prevents disputes over what was agreed.

  • Extracts deliverables, naira deposits, milestones and refund rules from a pasted chat log
  • Confirms by SMS OTP and seals the terms with a SHA-256 hash, so any change shows up
  • Shares the sealed record on WhatsApp and logs receipts and photos as proof
  • Email sign-in code, with no password required

SkillBridge

  • Pricing engine works out the exact cost, customer quote and profit
  • Shareable quote card sent on WhatsApp, which the client can accept online
